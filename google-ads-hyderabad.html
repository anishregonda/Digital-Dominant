Google Ads questions

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your area, your services and how many people search for them. On a free 30-minute call we suggest a sensible starting budget for your goals. You pay it directly to Google, see what comes in, then decide. You can change or pause the budget any time.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. Our fee pays for the work. Your ad budget goes from your own Google Ads account straight to Google, so you can see every charge. We never mark it up.

How soon will I get calls?

Ads can start showing as soon as Google approves them, which usually takes about a working day. How many calls you get depends on your area, your budget and how many people are searching. We report real numbers after 30 days of tracking.

Can you guarantee the top spot or a number of leads?

No one can honestly guarantee that, because Google runs an auction for every search. What we promise is the work: weekly cleanup, clear tracking and a monthly report you can understand.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account is in your business's name and we manage it through our manager account. If you stop working with us, you keep the account and all its history.
