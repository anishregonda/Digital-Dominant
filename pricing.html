Questions about plans

Why don't you show prices?

Because every business needs a different mix. A salon with one branch and a clinic with three need different things, and so do their ad budgets. We quote after a free 30-minute call, so you only pay for what helps.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. Monthly care runs month to month and you can cancel any month. Just tell us.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay Google or Meta directly, from your own ad account, so you can see every charge. We suggest a budget that fits your goals, we never mark it up, and you can raise, lower or pause it any time.

Who owns my Google profile and ad accounts?

You do. They stay in your business's name, and we're added as a manager. If you ever leave, you keep them, with all their history.

Do you work outside Hyderabad?

Yes. We're a Hyderabad-based team, and everything we do (websites, Google profiles, ads and reports) works remotely. Book a call at a time that suits you, wherever you are.

How long does setup take?

Usually 6–10 days, counted from when we have what we need from you: your logo, photos, list of services and access to your Google profile.

What if I already have a website?

Send us the link. If it's fast and works well on a phone, we'll keep it and connect the ads and tracking to it. If it's slow or hard to use, we'll show you why and suggest a rebuild. Either way, you'll see the cost in writing before we start.

What is the DD Hub app?

Your own dashboard link, which you can add to your phone's home screen like an app. It shows your website visits, call and WhatsApp taps, enquiries, and your Google rating and reviews in one place. See DD Hub.
