Pricing questions

Is the Google ad spend included in the price?

No. You pay Google directly for your ads, from your own Google Ads account. We suggest starting with ₹5,000 or more a month. You can raise, lower or pause it any time.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. The monthly plan runs month to month and you can cancel any month. Just tell us on WhatsApp.

How long does setup take?

Your website and ads are ready in 6–10 days, counted from when we have what we need from you: logo, photos, services, prices and access to your Google profile.

What if I already have a website?

Send us the link. If it's fast and works well on a phone, we'll keep it and connect the ads and tracking to it. If it's slow or hard to use, we'll show you why and suggest a rebuild. You'll know the price before we start.

Do your prices include GST?

No. All prices are exclusive of taxes. GST is added if applicable.

What is the DD Hub app?

Your own dashboard link, which you can add to your phone's home screen like an app. It shows your website visits, call and WhatsApp taps, enquiries, and your Google rating and reviews in one place.
