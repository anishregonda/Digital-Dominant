How much does it cost?

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local businesses of all kinds: salons, clinics, shops, restaurants, service businesses and more. Right now we work with a cloth store and a salon in Hyderabad.

What timeframe can I expect when designing a new site?

Your new website is designed, built and launched in 6 to 10 days, counted from when we have your logo, photos, services and prices.

Will my site be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. Every site we build is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices from the ground up.

What should a local business website include?

A local business website must include strong calls to action, prominent contact information, clear service lists, high-quality images of past work, client testimonials, and a mobile-optimized layout to capture leads effectively.
