Questions about plans

Why don’t you show prices?

Because a three-page landing-page rebuild with one quote tool and a 20-page site with custom tools are not the same job, and neither are their ad budgets. Every engagement is scoped on the call: site size, ad spend, markets. You’ll have a written quote within 24 hours, one number per phase.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

Phase 1 is a one-off build: half to start, half at launch. Phase 2 is a flat monthly fee, paid monthly in advance, and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice after month 3.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay Google and Meta directly, from your own ad accounts, so you see every charge. We suggest a budget that fits your goals, we never mark it up, and you can raise, lower or pause it any time.

Who owns the site, the code and the ad accounts?

You do. Code and content are yours, hosting and domain stay in your accounts, and your Google and Meta accounts stay in your business’s name with us added as a manager. If you ever leave, you keep all of it, with the history.

Do you work with businesses in the US?

Yes. We work remotely with owner-led businesses wherever they are, on calls, Zoom and WhatsApp, and we take calls on US hours. Book a time that suits you.

How long does the rebuild take?

Phase 1 usually runs 4–6 weeks from the day we have access and your content: logo, photos, service list and about two hours of your time in week 1. Weeks 1–2 also record your baseline numbers, so there is something real to report against.

What if we already have a website?

Run the free Growth Check on it first, then send us the link. If it loads under 2 seconds and converts over 5% of visitors, we’ll tell you so and only talk about the ads. If it’s slow or asks too much of visitors, we’ll show you why and quote a rebuild, in writing, before anything starts.

How does the guarantee work?

It applies to cost per lead, after 3 months of Phase 2, when we run both your site and your ads. If your cost per lead isn’t below today’s by the end of month 3, month 4 is free. We measure it in your portal against the baseline recorded in weeks 1–2.
