Who it's for

Local businesses whose customers search on their phones

A local business website has one job: help people decide, then get in touch. What that looks like depends on your business.

Salons & spas

Services and prices on one page, photos of your work, and a WhatsApp button to book a slot.

Clinics

Doctors, timings and treatments, a one-tap call button, and directions for first-time patients.

Shops

A photo catalogue of what's on your shelf, like the 53-fabric catalogue we built for a cloth store. Customers ask about any item on WhatsApp.

Restaurants & cafés

Your menu, timings and location, with buttons to call, get directions or order on WhatsApp.

Website design questions

How long does a website take?

6 to 10 days from when we have your logo, photos, services and prices. You'll see a preview on your phone before it goes live.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what your business needs. Your website is part of our setup, which also covers your Google Business Profile cleanup, a Google Ads campaign with call tracking and a review QR stand. We quote after a free 30-minute call, in a written one-page proposal.

Do you use WordPress or templates?

No. We build each site ourselves with modern web tools, so it loads fast on phones and there are no plugins to update or break.

Will my website show up on Google?

We set up what Google needs to understand your business: page titles, descriptions, business details and a link to your Google profile. Ranking in the free results takes time, and nobody can honestly guarantee a position. Google Ads can show you above the free results for chosen searches while that builds.

Can customers message me on WhatsApp from the site?

Yes. Every page has call and WhatsApp buttons, and the enquiry form sends messages straight to you. If the form ever fails, it opens WhatsApp instead, so no enquiry is lost.
