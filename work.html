Bhadrakali Cloth Store

Fabric shop · Miyapur, Hyderabad

The problem

A fabric shop selling sarees, dress materials and cloth by the metre, with no website of its own. People searching online couldn't see what was on the shelf, what it cost, or ask a quick question before making the trip.

What we did

  • Built a custom website with a catalogue of 53 fabrics, each with a photo, and prices where the shop wants them shown
  • Added WhatsApp enquiry buttons, so a customer can ask about a fabric in one tap
  • Set up Google Ads conversion tracking, so WhatsApp taps and counter bookings that come from an ad are counted
  • Put the site live on the shop's own domain

What's next

Google Ads on a small starting budget. After 30 days of tracking we'll add the real numbers here: visits, WhatsApp enquiries and what each one cost.

Hair Factor Unisex Salon

Salon · Begumpet, Hyderabad

The salon was running Google Ads, but there was no clear way to see which calls came from them. The owner wanted proof that the ad money was bringing in customers.

What we did

  • Rebuilt the Search campaign around 42 keywords
  • Added sitelinks and callouts, so the ad shows more links and more reasons to choose the salon
  • Set up call tracking, so every call from an ad is counted
