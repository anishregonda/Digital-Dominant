We fix the thing the clicks land on

Digital Dominant is a technical growth partner for owner-led service businesses. We rebuild your website as a fast app with a built-in lead qualifier, run Google and Meta ads into it, and report cost per lead weekly in your own portal.

How we work

You get the two people who build and run it

  • No account manager. You talk to the people doing the work, on calls, WhatsApp or Zoom, wherever you are.
  • Weekly numbers, good or bad. Cost per lead is the number we report on.
  • Ad spend stays in your accounts. Billed by Google and Meta to you, never through us, never marked up.
  • Code and content are yours. Hosting, domain and ad accounts stay in your name.

Most agencies sell you clicks

Your site loads in five seconds on a phone and asks every visitor to fill in a form, so most of the clicks you pay for leave before they ever talk to you. The ad was never the problem. The page was.

So we fix the thing the clicks land on. We rebuild it as a fast Next.js app with a 3-question quote tool, route each lead to your phone in seconds, run your Google and Meta ads into it, and show you spend, leads and cost per lead in a portal we built, not a PDF once a month.

Our Story

Digital Dominant started by building websites and running Google Ads for owner-led businesses, and by building DD Hub, our own portal, because a monthly report of impressions and clicks never told an owner what a lead actually cost.

Today we run the whole system as one: the page, the ads and the follow-up. We don’t just build websites; we build lead-capture apps, the software around them, and the reporting that shows whether they work, so you can get back to running your business.
