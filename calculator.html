What the numbers mean

The arithmetic, in plain words

Conversion rate

The share of visitors who do the thing you want: call, book, or send a form. If 2,000 people visit and 40 enquire, that’s 2%. Your ads account or analytics shows it; if not, we measure it in weeks 1–2.

Why double is a fair target

A fast page with a 3-question tool removes the two biggest reasons people leave: the wait and the form. We start from double your current rate and adjust once your own numbers come in. Move the slider to whatever you believe.

Same traffic, same spend

The calculator changes nothing about your ad budget. The extra leads come from the visitors you already pay for, which is why cost per lead falls as conversion rises.

It’s an estimate, not a promise

The result is your inputs multiplied together. Real results depend on your market, your offer and how fast leads are followed up. We report the real numbers weekly in your portal once we start.
